# Features

## Moment creation

The moment form captures event, mood, relationship context, soundtrack style, story, partner nickname, and optional photo.

## Discovery

The moment automatically generates a discovery pack for:

- YouTube songs
- Spotify songs
- Poetry
- Wise quotes
- Social captions
- Broad web search

## Advanced emotional assistance

The app is designed for people who have difficulty expressing their search request. It detects common emotional situations from natural language and adds useful search concepts.

## Local memories

Moments are stored in browser localStorage. There is no remote database.

## Export

Users can export saved memories as JSON.

## Local AI preparation

The app creates prompts suitable for local/browser AI systems. It does not embed a private cloud AI key.

## Song recognition recording

The browser can record an audio clip. Recognition requires a separately configured recognition provider because a static site cannot safely hide the provider's private credential.
